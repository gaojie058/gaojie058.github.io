I am fundamentally fascinated by how people identify patterns and derive reusable principles from messy, ambiguous, and complex situations. Text and code are my entry points into these complex worlds, situations, and phenomena. This is why I am drawn to analytical methods such as thematic analysis, grounded theory, content analysis, and taxonomy building. These methods help people turn complexity into understanding.

I am equally interested in making these ways of understanding more accessible. My research explores how analytical processes can be simplified, supported, and partially automated, often with AI, so that more people can make sense of complex situations without losing human judgment and reasoning.

Across tasks such as analyzing unstructured text, understanding codebases, and understanding AI agents, I design and evaluate human-AI systems that help people build more understandable, human-readable interpretations of complex information.

I use tools to bring my design ideas to life. Building software that solves real problems genuinely makes me happy. My latest tool is mindcoder.ai.

I am mostly interested in two complementary directions:

Bring AI to Humans

Integrating AI into human workflows to help people perform subjective tasks more effectively and confidently.

AI-Assisted Qualitative Analysis Codebase Onboarding/Understanding Developer Experience

Bring Humans to AI

Bringing humans to AI to ensure better supervision and evaluation of increasingly autonomous AI systems, so that these systems are more trustworthy and responsible.
