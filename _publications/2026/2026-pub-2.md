abstract: >-
  The paper introduces Act*ONOMY, a hierarchical taxonomy for analyzing autonomous agent behavior during runtime. The framework consists of 10 actions, 46 subactions, and 120 leaf categories, structured to help researchers understand agent trajectories and execution traces. The authors present an open repository with an automated analysis pipeline and extension protocol, demonstrating how the taxonomy can identify behavioral patterns and failure modes across different agents.
