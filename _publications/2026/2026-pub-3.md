abstract: >-
  Agent-as-Peer-Debriefer brings the human qualitative-analysis practice of peer debriefing into multi-agent qualitative data analysis. Peer agents apply theory-driven, data-driven, and applied perspectives to review and refine an initial agent's codes. Across three datasets, two domains, and three language models, perspective-based review aligns more closely with human annotations than a single-agent baseline.
