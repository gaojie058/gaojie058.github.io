abstract: >-
  This paper adapts grounded theory for analyzing large collections of agent trajectories and introduces AutoTraceGT, a multi-agent pipeline that conducts open, axial, and theoretical coding until saturation. Across six trajectory corpora, its resulting codebooks recover most failure modes in human-annotated taxonomies and surface additional behavioral patterns.
