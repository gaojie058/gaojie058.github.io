# Photo credits

Every photo here comes from Wikimedia Commons and may be republished, but each one
carries a licence. To use one on the page, point `hero.image.src` in `_data/monash.yml`
at its filename and paste that photo's `credit:` block from below. The credit line is
not decoration: for the CC BY and CC BY-SA photos it is the condition of use.

Keep each `credit:` value on ONE line. The block uses YAML folded style (`>-`), where a
line break becomes a space, so wrapping a URL would break the link.

Do not add a photo to this folder from monash.edu, Google Images, or a stock site
without first checking that you may republish it. Most of those you may not.
